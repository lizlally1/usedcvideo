## Scene 1 — Opening (0:00–0:12 / 0–360f)

- **Visuals:** Cold open on `armour-exterior.mp4` (first ~3s), slow push-in.
  Cut on a soft cross-dissolve to a 1.5s static crop of the branded
  "U.S. ENERGY DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION" sign (from `hq-reception-reveal.mp4`)
  under the logo/title reveal. Navy gradient vignette top and bottom for
  text legibility.
- **On-screen text:** Title card fades up — "Built on Experience. Driven by
  What's Next." — with "U.S. Energy Development Corporation" as a smaller
  supporting line beneath. Both fade/slide out before frame 360.
- **Audio:** Music fades in quietly from frame 0. Narration begins ~frame 90.
- **Transition out:** dip-to-navy into Scene 2.

## Scene 2 — The Beginning (0:12–0:33 / 360–990f)

- **Visuals:** Navy background with a subtle slow-zoom still
  (`bg-office.jpg`, heavily tinted/blurred, low opacity) for texture. Large
  "1980" numeral animates in (gold), timeline line draws in beneath it.
- **On-screen text:** "1980" (large, gold) → "A Family Vision Takes Shape"
  (condensed display, white).
- **Audio:** Narration: founding story (Buffalo, NY, Appalachian roots).
- **Transition out:** timeline line extends and leads into Scene 3.

## Scene 3 — Growth Through the Decades (0:33–0:57 / 990–1710f)

- **Visuals:** Horizontal timeline component sweeps across, four markers
  animate in sequence: 1985, 1990s, 2014, 2015. `bg-armour-exterior.jpg`
  as a soft parallax backdrop layer.
- **On-screen text (one at a time, milestone + 2-4 word label):**
  - "1985 — Appalachian Development"
  - "1990s — Multi-State Expansion"
  - "2014 — Second-Generation Leadership"
  - "2015 — Growth in Texas"
- **Audio:** Narration: expansion into TX/LA/KS, 2014 leadership, 2015 Texas.
- **Transition out:** timeline collapses into a single gold line that
  becomes the baseline for Scene 4's statistics.

## Scene 4 — Success and Experience (0:57–1:19 / 1710–2370f)

- **Visuals:** `bg-hq-reception.jpg` soft slow-zoom backdrop. Statistic
  component presents two, then a third, large confident numerals.
- **On-screen text (max 2–3 at once):**
  - "~4,000 Wells"
  - "13 States + Canada"
  - "Billions Deployed"
  - then, alone: "2025 — A Transformational Year" / "Largest Acquisition in Company History"
- **Audio:** Narration: current scale statistics, 2025 acquisition.
- **Transition out:** cross-dissolve into live office footage.

## Scene 5 — The People and Core Values (1:19–1:51 / 2370–3330f)

- **Visuals:** `office-culture-hoops.mp4` (full clip, ~6.2s) — employees
  laughing and playing in the open office. Cross-dissolve into
  `core-values-wall.mp4`, split into three sequential sub-ranges as the
  camera continues its single push across the physical values wall.
- **On-screen text (lower-third, one value at a time, never more than one):**
  - Over office-culture clip: "Collaborative" / "Strong partnerships begin with strong teams."
  - Over values-wall segment 1: "Trustworthy & Sincere"
  - Over values-wall segment 2: "Innovative"
  - Over values-wall segment 3: "Passionate & Driven"
- **Audio:** Narration: "it's the people... collaborative, sincere, driven... investors come first... partners like family."
- **Transition out:** cross-dissolve, dip to navy.

## Scene 6 — The Fort Worth Stockyards (1:51–2:12 / 3330–3960f)

- **Visuals:** `hq-reception-reveal.mp4` (full walk-in reveal of the branded
  reception nook) cross-dissolves into `elevator-archival-mural.mp4` (doors
  opening onto the "Armour & Company" archival mural) — the door-opening
  motion is used as the scene's natural internal transition.
- **On-screen text:** "Fort Worth, Texas" → "A New Headquarters. The Next
  Chapter."
- **Audio:** Narration: 2025 headquarters move into the historic Armour
  Building.
- **Transition out:** slow dip-to-navy.

## Scene 7 — Conclusion (2:12–2:31 / 3960–4530f)

- **Visuals:** Short montage: `armour-exterior.mp4` (different, later
  sub-range than Scene 1 — the walker closer to camera, smiling), then a
  final held wide shot of the building exterior with a subtle slow zoom-out.
- **On-screen text:** "45+ Years of Energy and Impact" → "Experience Behind
  Us." / "Opportunity Ahead." → logo mark + "usedc.com".
- **Audio:** Narration concludes ("the opportunity is just getting
  started"). Music swells gently then fades out over the final 3 seconds.

## Design language recap

- Navy (#00012A) dominant background; dark red (#82171A) and gold used only
  for timeline markers, dividers, and key words.
- Condensed display font for titles/dates; clean sans-serif for supporting
  copy and captions. Two font families total.
- Lower-thirds: white text over a soft transparent navy gradient, generous
  padding, title-safe margins respected on all sides.
- Transitions: cross-dissolves, dip-to-navy, and the two "real" match cuts
  (door reveal, elevator reveal) already present in the footage. No spin,
  glitch, or whip-zoom transitions anywhere in the edit.
