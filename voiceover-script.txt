SCENE 1 — THE RISE OF THE FORT WORTH STOCKYARDS (0:00–0:27)
--------------------------------------------------
By the late 1800s, the Fort Worth Stockyards had become one of America's
most important livestock trading centers. Railroads transformed Fort Worth
into the gateway of the cattle industry, earning the city its legendary
reputation as Cowtown. For generations, millions of cattle passed through
these historic grounds, helping shape the economy of Texas and the
American West.

--------------------------------------------------
SCENE 2 — THE ARMOUR BUILDING (0:27–1:01)
--------------------------------------------------
In the early twentieth century, the Armour Building was constructed to
serve Armour and Company, one of the nation's largest meatpacking
operations. Built with remarkable craftsmanship and designed to support
the thriving livestock industry, the building became an enduring symbol of
the Stockyards' industrial legacy.

Even more remarkably, many of the craftsmen whose families originally laid
the building's brick generations ago returned decades later to assist in
its careful reconstruction during U.S. Energy's renovation — helping
preserve an important piece of Fort Worth history.

--------------------------------------------------
SCENE 3 — HONORING THE ORIGINAL STRUCTURE (1:01–1:34)
--------------------------------------------------
Throughout the renovation, preserving the building's historic character
remained a guiding principle. One of its most distinctive architectural
features — the original white structural beams — remain exactly as they
were when the building was first constructed. They continue to tell the
story of the craftsmanship that has endured for more than a century.

Today, the Armour Building also features a state-of-the-art fitness center
with a dedicated personal trainer, welcoming employees of both the Armour
Building and U.S. Energy, along with members of the Fort Worth Police
Department.

--------------------------------------------------
SCENE 4 — A NEW HOME FOR U.S. ENERGY (1:34–2:25)
--------------------------------------------------
Today, the Armour Building stands as the proud home of U.S. Energy
Development Corporation — a place where history and innovation come
together.

More than simply an office, this headquarters serves as a destination for
investors, partners, and clients visiting one of Texas' most iconic
landmarks. Elegant conference rooms and versatile event spaces create an
environment where relationships are built, ideas are shared, and
opportunities take shape.

As we continue growing, this remarkable building reflects who we are:
honoring our heritage while investing boldly in the future. We believe
this headquarters is more than a workplace — it is a beacon that welcomes
clients, inspires employees, and positions U.S. Energy for continued
success for generations to come.
